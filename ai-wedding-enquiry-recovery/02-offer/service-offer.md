## 2. Where ₹799 comes from (the price logic you can say out loud)

- A single Indian wedding photography booking typically runs **₹30,000 to ₹1,30,000+** (published 2026 price guides — see `01-research-and-validation/market-research.md` §1.2).
- ₹799 is roughly **0.6%–2.7% of one booking**.
- The nearest paid alternatives: photographer CRMs at ~**US$16–49/month** (₹1,400–₹4,200/month, in dollars, requiring self-setup), a Fiverr CRM setup at about **US$80** (~₹7,000, and it assumes you already pay for a CRM), and wedding-portal listings at **₹50,000–₹3,60,000** (vendor-reported).

**Say this, not promises:** *"This is priced as a pilot, not as a product. I keep it at ₹799 because I want a first client and honest feedback."*

**Never say:** "This will pay for itself" (as a certainty), "guaranteed ROI", or anything implying bookings.

## 3. Is the proposed pilot (20 enquiries for ₹799) practical? — Our verdict

**The original draft said "up to 20 enquiries". We are cutting it to 15, with a hard cap of 30 message drafts.**

Why:

| Factor | Reality |
|---|---|
| Workload at 20 enquiries | Sorting + classifying 20 + drafting 2 messages for ~10 + workflow + report ≈ **11–14 hours** |
| Workload at 15 enquiries | ≈ **8–11 hours** (see `01-research-and-validation/idea-assessment.md` §5) |
| Effective rate at 20 for ₹799 | ≈ **₹57–₹73/hour** — below minimum-wage territory and a path to burnout |
| Effective rate at 15 for ₹799 | ≈ **₹73–₹100/hour** — still low, but this is *deliberately* a pilot price, and 15 is a deliverable workload in a week alongside a normal life |
| Quality risk | Past 15 enquiries, message quality drops, mistakes rise, and the 7-day promise breaks |

**Decision: 15 enquiries maximum, 30 drafts maximum.** This keeps the 7-day promise honest, protects quality, and creates a clean reason to offer a paid extension to bigger studios instead of quietly overworking yourself.

**Extension (optional, only if the client asks):** +10 more enquiries for **+₹399**, delivered within 4 working days after the first handover. This is a **separate purchase**, not part of the ₹799 Sprint. Never include it by default.

## 4. Exact deliverables

All six items are delivered **inside one Google Drive folder** (or Google Sheet shared with them), plus one email summary.

| # | Deliverable | Format | What's inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **Enquiry Tracker** | Google Sheet (also works in Excel) | 13 columns, dropdown statuses, conditional colour, filters, due-date flags, auto-calculated "days since last contact", and a dashboard tab with counts by stage. Pre-filled with their enquiries (up to 15) |
| **D2** | **Status & Next-Action Framework** | One-page reference inside the tracker | 8 statuses explained + a "if X then do Y" decision table so they never ask "what now?" again |
| **D3** | **Personalised Follow-Up Drafts** | Google Doc — copy-paste blocks | Up to **30** message drafts personalised to the actual enquiry (their names/initials, event date, what was already discussed, style/pricing mentioned, tone). Each draft is labelled with which enquiry it belongs to and which day to send it |
| **D4** | **7-Day Follow-Up Workflow** | 2-page PDF-style document | The day-by-day process: review → sort → decide → draft → approve → send → log. Includes the "stop rule" for anyone who has declined or opted out |
| **D5** | **Handover Guide** | 3-page document + a 15-minute walkthrough voice note or call | How to use the tracker, how to reuse the drafts, how to keep it alive after 7 days, what to do when a new enquiry arrives, and how to handle WhatsApp/Instagram/email consistently |
| **D6** | **Results Summary & Recommendations** | 1–2 page document | Only when real data is available (i.e. after the client reports outcomes at the Day-14 check-in). States facts: enquiries reviewed, drafts approved and sent, replies observed, bookings observed — then 3–5 process suggestions. **No invented numbers ever.** |

**Also included at no extra charge:** one round of revisions (see §8), and a **Day-14 check-in** (a 10-minute message exchange to log outcomes).

## 5. What is explicitly NOT included

State this in writing before payment. It prevents 90% of disputes.

- ❌ Sending messages on the client's behalf — **we never contact their couples.** They send; we draft.
- ❌ Bulk messaging, WhatsApp broadcasting, or WhatsApp automation ("sender" tools, mods, unofficial APIs) — these violate Meta's policy and risk a number ban.
- ❌ Lead generation, ads, SEO, or any promise of new enquiries.
- ❌ Setting up or paying for any CRM (HoneyBook, Dubsado, Studio Ninja, Pixieset, Táve, etc.), or any paid software at all.
- ❌ Website, portfolio, logo or Instagram redesign.
- ❌ Photo or video editing, album design, culling, or colour grading.
- ❌ Contracts, invoices to their couples, GST work, or legal/tax advice.
- ❌ Calling or messaging their clients for any reason.
- ❌ Chasing unpaid invoices from *their* clients.
- ❌ Handling more than 15 enquiries, more than 30 drafts, or more than one brand voice.
- ❌ Any guarantee of bookings, revenue, response rates, or "recovered" money.
- ❌ Ongoing management after day 14 (that would be a different, monthly service — not sold yet).

**Polite ways to say no** are in `06-sales-system/objection-handling.md`.

## 6. Delivery timeline (the 7 days)

Reference: **T** = the day the advance payment is received **and** the completed onboarding questionnaire / enquiry list is received. Delivery starts the next working day after both.

| Day | What happens | Who | Time needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T-0** | Payment received (₹400). Onboarding questionnaire + enquiry list received in the agreed format | Client | 15–20 min |
| **T+1** | Acknowledge & confirm scope. Set up the tracker shell and import their enquiries | You | ~1.5 h |
| **T+2** | Classify every enquiry: status, next action, follow-up due date, priority | You | ~2 h |
| **T+3** | Send **first draft batch** (up to 15 drafts) for approval | You → client | ~2 h |
| **T+4** | Client approves/edits batch 1. You send **second draft batch** (up to 15 follow-up drafts) | Both | ~2 h + client 20 min |
| **T+5** | Build the workflow + handover guide, personalised to their style | You | ~1.5 h |
| **T+6** | Final quality check; fix anything flagged; send the complete package | You | ~1 h |
| **T+7** | **Handover:** 15-min walkthrough (call or voice note + questions). Balance ₹399 due on handover | Both | 20–30 min |
| **T+14** | Check-in: log outcomes into the tracker, then produce **D6 Results Summary** | You | 30 min |

**If something slips:** tell the client *before* the deadline, in one line, with the new date. Never go silent — silence is what ruins small service businesses. Two smooth days late beats a botched on-time delivery.

**Realistic client inaction risk:** if the client doesn't send the list or approve drafts within 3 days, the timeline pauses and resumes when they do. Write this in the agreement (it is standard for a 7-day service).

## 7. Information needed from the client (keep it minimal — privacy by design)

Ask for exactly this, nothing more. **No phone numbers. No email addresses. No couple photos.**

| # | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A list of up to **15 open/recent enquiries** — from their WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, email, or portal, copied into a Google Sheet or a plain document | We cannot organise what we cannot see |
| 2 | For each enquiry: **first name or initials**, enquiry source, when they enquired, event date, what was discussed/sent | Enough to classify and personalise, without exposing a couple's identity |
| 3 | Their **price list / packages** (or a summary of the ranges they quote) | So drafts never contradict their prices |
| 4 | Their **tone preference**: formal, warm, casual, Hinglish-friendly — plus 2–3 examples of messages they already send | Drafts must sound like them, not like a robot |
| 5 | Their **available dates / how they handle date availability** | So a draft doesn't ignore that a date is already booked |
| 6 | Their **business name and preferred sign-off** | Consistency |
| 7 | Written confirmation that they have the couples' consent/permission basis to have this data processed on their behalf | Protects everyone (DPDP Act — see `privacy-and-data-handling.md`) |

**Provided via:** `04-brand-and-sales/onboarding-questionnaire.md` (a Google Form or a copy-paste Doc).

## 8. Responsibilities

**The service provider (you) will:**
1. Deliver all six items within 7 days of receiving payment + complete input.
2. Personalise drafts to the client's actual enquiries and tone.
3. Reply to questions within 24 hours on weekdays.
4. Keep client data confidential, store it only in the client's own Google account where possible, and delete personal copies within 30 days.
5. Tell the client immediately if any input is unclear or missing rather than guessing.
6. Never claim results, and never invent numbers in the report.

**The client (photographer) will:**
1. Provide the completed questionnaire and the enquiry list within 48 hours of paying the advance.
2. Provide the list in the agreed simple format (a sheet or a document with the required fields).
3. Reply to draft-approval messages within 48 hours each time.
4. **Read every draft and decide whether to send it.** They are the sender and the responsible party.
5. Not ask for messages to people who have asked not to be contacted.
6. Pay the balance ₹399 at handover.

**Shared responsibility:** deciding what is appropriate to send to a particular couple. We draft; the client judges.

## 12. What "success" means for this service

Set this expectation **before** payment, using these exact ideas:

> *"Success for the Sprint is: you have every open enquiry in one place, you know the next action for each one, you have drafts ready to send in your own voice, and you have a 7-day habit you can actually keep. If you can open your tracker any morning and know the three people to message today, the Sprint worked."*

| Measured by (objective) | Not measured by (out of our control) |
|---|---|
| Tracker delivered within 7 days | Number of bookings |
| Every included enquiry has a status + next action + due date | Revenue "recovered" |
| Up to 30 drafts delivered and approved | Reply rate from couples |
| Client can use the tracker unaided after the handover call | Client's conversion rate |
| Client sends at least one follow-up they wouldn't have sent before | Whether a couple says yes |

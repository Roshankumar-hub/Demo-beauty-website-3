# The 7-Day Follow-Up Workflow

This is the process the client keeps. It is deliberately small: **two work blocks a week plus a 5-minute daily check.**

**Time needed:** about 30 minutes on Day 1, then ~10–15 minutes a day, then 30 minutes on Day 7. Total under 2.5 hours for the week.

---

## The whole system in 5 lines

1. **One place** for every enquiry (the tracker).
2. **Every enquiry has a status and a next action.**
3. **Two fixed blocks** a week where all messages get written and sent.
4. **A 5-minute daily glance** at only the `P1 - today` rows.
5. **A stop rule**: maximum 3 follow-ups, then close it.

---

## Day 1 (30 min) — Review and organise

| Step | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Open the tracker. Any enquiry from the last 90 days that isn't booked or closed goes in. | 5 min |
| 1.2 | One row per enquiry. Use identifiers only — initials or first names. Never phone numbers. | 10 min |
| 1.3 | For each row, fill: Received Date, Requested Service, Event Date, Enquiry Source. | 5 min |
| 1.4 | Set Enquiry Status for every row using the Status Guide. | 5 min |
| 1.5 | For each row, set Next Action and Follow-Up Due Date (see the rules below). | 5 min |

**Follow-up due dates (defaults — judgment always wins):**

| Status | Default follow-up due |
|---|---|
| New enquiry (never answered) | **Today** — reply within 24 hours, ideally same day |
| Clarification needed | Today or tomorrow |
| Call requested | Today |
| Quote sent | 3 days after the quote |
| Awaiting reply | 3–4 days after your last message |
| Deciding / comparing | 3 days |
| Call scheduled | 1 day before the call |
| Booked / Closed | No follow-up needed |
| Dormant - no reply | Only if you want to send one final message — otherwise leave it |

**Channel rule:** always reply on the channel where they contacted you *and* note it in the tracker. If they messaged on Instagram, reply on Instagram (then offer WhatsApp for convenience if they prefer).

---

## Day 1 (10 min) — Send today's messages first

You will be tempted to spend all your time organising and then never send anything. **Send the replies to unanswered enquiries first.** Organisation with no messages is a hobby, not a system.

Priority order every single day:

1. **New enquiries that never got a reply** (`New enquiry`) — template 1.
2. **Anything overdue** (`P1 - today`) — the matching template.
3. **Anything due today** (`K = Due today`).
4. Everything else can wait.

---

## Days 2–6 — The 5-minute daily glance

Every morning, open the tracker and:

- [ ] Filter **Priority = `P1 - today`** → handle those rows first (usually 0–3 items).
- [ ] Filter **Enquiry Status = `New enquiry`** → reply to all of them (template 1).
- [ ] Update **Last Contact Date** for anything you messaged.
- [ ] Set the next **Follow-Up Due Date** (+3 days for a nudge, +7 for the next one).

That's it. Nothing else needs daily attention. Do **not** scroll the whole sheet — the whole point of the tracker is that you don't have to think.

---

## Day 3 (20 min) — Follow-up block #1

Work only rows where the follow-up is due today or is overdue:

| Their situation | Template to use |
|---|---|
| Sent a quote, no reply, 3–4 days | 2 — quote follow-up (include a similar wedding) |
| Asked a question, no reply | 4 — "your answer may have got buried" |
| Asked for a call, no slot confirmed | 3 — confirm the call slot |
| Confused about inclusions | 5 — clarify the package in numbered lines |
| Said "still deciding" | 6 — low-pressure, offer a short hold |
| Declined / booked elsewhere | 8 — graceful close (then stop) |

**Rules for this block:**
- Send a maximum of **3 follow-ups per enquiry in total** — count them.
- Never two messages to the same person on the same day.
- After sending, update: `Last Contact Date`, `Follow-Up Status = Completed`, `Follow-Up Due Date = +7 days`, and if the follow-up count reaches 3, change the status accordingly.

---

## Day 5 (20 min) — Follow-up block #2

Same as Day 3, for anything that became due.

Anything that has now had **3 follow-ups with no reply** → mark it:

- `Enquiry Status = Dormant - no reply`
- `Next Action = No action needed`
- `Follow-Up Status = No follow-up needed`

**One exception:** if the event date is more than 3 months away, one *final* polite message (template 7) is allowed. Then close it.

---

## Day 7 (30 min) — Review and handover (do this every week, forever)

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 7.1 | Open the Dashboard tab. Note: total enquiries, open, overdue, booked, and weddings in the next 30 days. |
| 7.2 | For each enquiry, write the honest Current Stage in the Outcome/Notes column. |
| 7.3 | Ask: **which enquiries never got a reply at all?** That is the single number that matters most. If it's above zero, the daily glance slipped. |
| 7.4 | Ask: **where do enquiries come from, and which source actually converts?** After 4–6 weeks you'll see a pattern — most photographers find 1–2 sources produce most of their bookings. |
| 7.5 | Move every Booked and Closed enquiry out of view (filter or delete after archiving). Keep the tracker to open items only. |
| 7.6 | Plan next week's two follow-up blocks in your calendar. Put them in as recurring 20-minute appointments. |

---

## The stop rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Someone says no, "not interested", "we've booked someone", "stop messaging me":** stop. Immediately. Mark `Closed - not proceeding`. Never message that enquiry again — not in six months, not ever, unless *they* write first.
2. **3 follow-ups after your first reply.** Then stop. Silence is an answer.
3. **Never two messages in one day** to the same person, on any channel.
4. **Never price-related follow-ups more than twice.** They have your price. Repeating it is pressure, not service.
5. **Never message a couple's family members, wedding planners, or friends** to "get through to them".
6. **Never message from a second number or a new account** if someone has blocked you. That is harassment.
7. **If they say "not now, after the wedding season"** — note the exact month they mentioned, and message once at that time. Not before.

## Small habits that make this survive contact with real life

- **Do the messages before the admin.** Every day.
- **Reply on the same channel** they used. Couples who DM don't check email.
- **Add new enquiries the same day they arrive.** A tracker that is two days stale is a tracker nobody trusts.
- **Voice notes beat long typing** for warm Indian clients — a 30-second voice note often gets a faster reply than a paragraph. Only after they've shown interest, never as a first cold message.
- **Share the tracker with the client's team** (if they have one) so anyone answering a DM updates the same sheet. One owner per enquiry.
- **Never delete an enquiry row silently.** If someone books elsewhere, mark it — that data tells you *why* you lose enquiries, which is worth more than the row.

---

## What this workflow will NOT do

- It will not make couples reply.
- It will not make people book.
- It will not replace a portfolio, pricing, or reputation.

**What it will do:** make sure nobody is forgotten, everyone gets an answer, and no follow-up depends on your memory at 11 pm after a shoot. That is the entire promise.

## 6. Suggested process improvements

1. **Daily 5-minute glance at P1 rows.** The tracker flagged 4 overdue follow-ups on delivery day. Most of the lost momentum was 3–7 day silence, not decisions against the studio.
2. **Answer specific questions in numbered lines.** Two enquiries stalled because a question about album inclusion was answered vaguely twice. The clarification draft fixed both.
3. **Reply on Instagram to Instagram enquiries**, then offer WhatsApp. Instagram DMs held the highest volume and the lowest reply rate in this data.
4. **Close enquiries cleanly.** Set `Closed` or `Dormant` instead of leaving rows open — it keeps the P1 list honest and stops unpressured, unnecessary messaging.
5. **Send a thank-you to declined enquiries.** One of them explicitly said "stay in touch for 2027". That is a future booking sitting in an old chat.
6. **Add a 2-line enquiry summary to every new chat** as soon as it arrives (date, venue, service, source). It takes 20 seconds and it is what makes the weekly review possible.

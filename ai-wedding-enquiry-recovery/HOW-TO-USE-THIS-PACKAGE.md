# How To Use This Package

A guided walkthrough. Read this once, then work from the checklists.

**Total time to be ready to sell: about 6 hours across two days.**
**Money required: ₹0.**

---

## The three rules

1. **Outreach before perfection.** Everything here already works. Do not rewrite documents when you could be sending messages.
2. **Spend nothing.** Not on domains, tools, ads, courses, or logos. Revisit spending only after ₹799 has actually landed in your account.
3. **Tell the truth, always.** You are a new service with no clients yet. Saying that plainly will win you more business than any claim ever will — and it means you never have to remember a lie.

---

## Phase 1 — Understand what you're selling (≈90 minutes)

Do this today. Everything else depends on it.

**Read, in this order:**

1. `02-offer/service-offer.md` — **the source of truth for the offer.** Read it twice.
   → *You should finish this able to say:* "₹799. Up to 15 enquiries organised, up to 30 follow-up drafts written. 7 days. You send every message."
2. `01-research-and-validation/idea-assessment.md` — the verdict, the risks, and the decision rules you commit to in advance.
3. `01-research-and-validation/market-research.md` §2 and §7 — the evidence, and the legal/policy context.
4. `01-research-and-validation/validation-experiment.md` — the 12 discovery questions.

**Then do this (30 minutes):**

5. Open `02-offer/privacy-and-data-handling.md` and read the **six rules**. These are the promises that make it easy for a photographer to say yes and hard for anything to go wrong.
6. Say your offer out loud, three times, without reading. If you stumble, read the offer again.
7. Write your **own** one-sentence version somewhere you'll see it.

**Output:** you can explain the offer, the exclusions, and the price from memory.

---

## Phase 2 — Set up your ₹0 business basics (≈45 minutes)

Do this before outreach, so you're never fumbling when a warm client is waiting.

1. **`07-payments-and-delivery/payment-guide-india.md` §5** — the 9-step setup.
2. Create a separate **business Gmail account**. Turn on 2-step verification.
3. **Verify your UPI works**: send yourself ₹1 from a second account and confirm it appears in your app.
   → *Do not skip this. Never tell anyone a payment method works until you have seen the money move.*
4. Create your payment ledger sheet (columns listed in the payment guide).
5. Create an invoice folder: `Invoices/2026/`.

**Output:** you can accept ₹400 today, and you have a place to record it.

---

## Phase 3 — Get your demo ready (≈90 minutes)

1. Open `03-demo-materials/enquiry-tracker/AWER-Enquiry-Tracker-SAMPLE.xlsx` (Excel) or upload it to Google Drive → open with Google Sheets.
2. Follow `03-demo-materials/enquiry-tracker/google-sheets-setup-guide.md` to recreate it in Google Sheets if you prefer.
3. Check it **on your phone**. If it's unreadable there, widen or hide columns until the four key columns fit.
4. Create one shareable **view** link for the sample (`Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer`).
   → *Safe, because the sample is fictional. Never share a real client's tracker publicly.*
5. Read `03-demo-materials/sample-client-report.md` and note how carefully it avoids claiming results.
6. Skim `03-demo-materials/message-templates/client-message-templates.md`. Rewrite **two** templates in your own words so you have something real to show.

**Optional but valuable (30 min):** do a dry run — invent 8 fictional enquiries and process them fully (sort → classify → write 12 drafts). Time it. If it takes more than 5 hours, your first client gets **12 enquiries**, not 15.

**Output:** a working sample you can send to anyone in one message.

---

## Phase 4 — Prepare to find people (≈45 minutes)

1. Read `05-prospecting/playbook.md`. Pick **two cities** to focus on.
2. Read `05-prospecting/outreach-scripts.md`. Adapt the first DM and first email into your own words. Save both as phone notes.
3. Open `05-prospecting/prospect-tracker.xlsx` and confirm the dropdowns work.
4. Read `06-sales-system/objection-handling.md` #1, #3, #4 and #5 — the four you'll hear most.

**Output:** scripts in your own voice, and a tracker ready to fill.

---

## Phase 5 — Build your 30-prospect list (≈3 hours, Day 3)

1. Research 30 prospects using Google Maps (15), Instagram hashtags (10), and search engines (5).
2. Score each one. Keep only those scoring 4+ (expect about 20).
3. Write a **specific observation** for each of the top 20 — about their *work* or their *process*, never about them personally.
4. Record the public contact route for each.

**Output:** 20 qualified photographers, each with a personalised opening line ready.

---

## Phase 6 — Sell (Days 4–7)

1. Work from `09-launch-plan/seven-day-launch-plan.md`.
2. Tick boxes in `09-launch-plan/seven-day-execution-checklist.md`.
3. Use `06-sales-system/sales-messages.md` for every reply.
4. Use `06-sales-system/objection-handling.md` when someone pushes back.
5. When someone says yes: `04-brand-and-sales/onboarding-questionnaire.md`, then `04-brand-and-sales/customer-agreement.md`, then `04-brand-and-sales/invoice-template.md`.

Work through `04-brand-and-sales/delivery-checklist.md` top to bottom. It is the single most useful file during delivery, and `07-payments-and-delivery/client-delivery-process.md` explains the how of each stage.

## The eight things you must do yourself (nobody can do these for you)

1. **Send the messages.** 10 a day for four days. This is the entire business.
2. **Talk to photographers.** 5+ real conversations.
3. **Ask for the money.** Say the number out loud: "₹799. Shall we do it? Here's the link."
4. **Paste your own real details** into every `[placeholder]` before sending anything to a client.
5. **Read every AI-generated draft before it reaches a client.** You are responsible for what goes out.
6. **Reject bad fits.** Tell people honestly when they don't need this.
7. **Deliver what you promised, in 7 days**, and tell the client *before* the deadline if you're slipping.
8. **Delete their data on time** (within 30 days) and confirm it in writing.

# Complete Sales Message Library

Every message you need, in the order you'll use them. Copy, replace the `[brackets]`, send.

> **Note on first-touch messages:** the Instagram DM, business email and website-form openers live in **`05-prospecting/outreach-scripts.md`** so there is only one source of truth for them. This file covers everything from "they replied" onward.

**Placeholders:** `[Name]` · `[Studio]` · `[City]` · `[Your name]` · `[your WhatsApp]` · `[your email]` · `[your UPI ID]` · `[link]`

      │

## Message 1 — They replied with a question you can answer in one line

> Hi `[Name]`, thanks for replying.
>
> `[Answer their question in one or two sentences, directly. No pitch.]`
>

**Principle:** answer first, sell second. Always.

>

>
> **What I do:** in 7 days, I take up to 15 of your existing enquiries and put them in one tracker — each with a status, the next action, and a follow-up date. Overdue ones flag themselves in red. Then I write up to **30 personalised follow-up messages** in your tone, tagged to each enquiry, so sending takes you 30 seconds. Finally, I show you a 5-minute-a-day routine to keep it going.
>
> **What I do not do:** generate leads, run ads, contact your clients, or promise bookings. You send every message.
>
> **Price:** ₹799 flat (₹400 now, ₹399 at handover). No software, no subscription.
>
> Want the sample? Two minutes to look at and you'll know whether it's worth it.

## Message 6 — Discovery questions (when they want to talk or aren't sure yet)

> Happy to talk it through. Before we do, three quick questions — they save us both time:
>
> 1. Roughly how many enquiries did you get in the last two months?
> 2. Of those, how many are still open — meaning not booked, not rejected, just not resolved?
> 3. Where do those enquiry details live right now: WhatsApp, Instagram, email, a notebook, or somewhere else?
>
> No wrong answers. If the number in question 2 is small, I'll tell you honestly that you probably don't need this yet.

**Then — and this is the important part — if their answer suggests they don't need it, say so and stop selling.** You will be remembered as the only person who stopped pitching.

## Message 12 — They said "maybe later, after the season"

> Fair enough — the season is not the time.
>
> When does it slow down for you? Tell me the month and I'll message you once then, and not before.
>
> Meanwhile, here's one thing you can do for free that takes two minutes: open your WhatsApp, search the name of any couple who received a quote more than 10 days ago and never replied, and send them the message below. It's the highest-value 30 seconds in your whole week.
>
> > *"Hi `[Name]`, I sent you my package details on `[date]` — just checking whether the dates still work for you, or if your plans have changed. Either way, no pressure at all. — `[Photographer]`"*
>
> If that feels useful, this is a small taste of what the full sprint does for 15 enquiries at once.

**This is the single highest-converting message in the library.** You give away real value, you demonstrate the product, and you leave the door open with a specific date.

## Message 15 — You need to delay delivery

> Hi `[Name]`, quick update and an apology: I need two extra days on your drafts. New date: `[date]`.
>
> I'd rather tell you now than deliver something rushed. Nothing changes on your side, and the balance stays due on handover.

**Always tell them before the deadline.** Silence damages trust far more than lateness.

## Message 16 — Follow-up after a "let me think about it" (3–4 days later)

> Hi `[Name]`, any thoughts on the sprint?
>
> If the hesitation is price, tell me and I'll be honest about whether it makes sense for you. If it's timing, tell me the month and I'll come back then. If it's that you're not sure this is a real problem for you, that's a completely valid answer too — I'd rather know.

**Naming the three real objections for them makes it easy to answer.** Silence is almost always one of those three.

## Timing rules for the whole system

| Situation | When to message |
|---|---|
| Their reply arrives | Within a few hours during business hours |
| First follow-up | 4 days after no reply |
| Second follow-up | **Never**, unless they replied |
| After a "let me think" | 3–4 days later (Message 16) |
| After "not now" | Once, on the date they named |
| After a no | Never |
| Delivery day | As promised, or earlier |
| Day-14 check-in | Exactly on day 14 |
| Deletion confirmation | Within 30 days, in writing |

**Best sending windows:** Tuesday–Thursday, 10 am–1 pm or 4–6 pm. Avoid weekends, festivals, and after 9 pm. Never send the same message twice in one day, on any channel.

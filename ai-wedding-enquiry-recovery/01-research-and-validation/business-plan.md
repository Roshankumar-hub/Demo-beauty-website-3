# Business Plan (90 Days) + Validation Checklist

Simple, one-file plan. Not a pitch deck. The point is to keep you from building things nobody asked for.

---

## 1. One-page business plan

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Business name** | AI Wedding Enquiry Recovery (working name — see `04-brand-and-sales/brand-guide.md`) |
| **What we sell** | A done-for-you **7-Day Enquiry Recovery Sprint**, ₹799, for independent wedding photographers and small studios in India |
| **The customer's problem we address** | Booking enquiries arrive across Instagram, WhatsApp, email and portals, get quotes, then go quiet — and nobody has a reliable list of who is owed which follow-up |
| **What we do NOT do** | We do not generate leads, run ads, message couples, edit photos, or guarantee bookings |
| **Why they'd choose us** | No software subscription, no setup learning, no sales pitch about "more leads" — they get a sorted tracker, written follow-ups, and a 7-day habit |
| **Price** | ₹799 flat (introductory). 50% advance, 50% on handover. No hidden charges |
| **Cost to run** | ₹0 to start (Google Sheets, Gmail, UPI, free website). Optional ≤₹1,000 only after the first payment |
| **Where customers come from** | Direct outreach: email + website contact forms + Instagram DMs + photographer communities. Later: referrals from delivered clients |
| **How we deliver** | Manual, templated, 6-step process (`07-payments-and-delivery/client-delivery-process.md`) |
| **Success measure for 90 days** | 3+ paid clients delivered, 1+ written testimonial, average delivery time under 6 hours, referral from at least 1 client |
| **Failure measure** | Fewer than 2 payments after 60 qualified conversations → change niche or offer (see decision rules) |
| **Biggest risk** | Outreach being ignored, and interest without payment |

### 90-day roadmap

| Days | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **1–7** | Launch attempt (see `09-launch-plan/seven-day-launch-plan.md`) | Offer finalised, samples ready, 40 prospects listed, 25+ contacted, 5+ conversations, 1 offer made, payment attempted |
| **8–14** | Land client #1 and deliver it | 1 delivered Sprint, honest feedback collected |
| **15–30** | Repeat what worked; systemise | 2nd client; delivery time down; objection list updated; tracker template improved |
| **31–60** | Raise quality, not features | 3rd client; price test for new clients after 3 deliveries; ask every client for one referral; publish one anonymised case study |
| **61–90** | Decide the real shape of the business | Choose: (a) stay a ₹799–₹1,999 service, (b) add a ₹99/month "keep-it-clean" subscription for past clients, (c) consider the free automation in `08-automation/`, or (d) stop and move to a better-fitting niche with what you learned |

**Rule for the whole 90 days:** no new feature, no new tool, no new niche until the current one has produced **three** delivered clients.

## 2. Validation checklist

Tick honestly. If something is unticked, it is an unvalidated assumption, not a fact.

### Level 1 — Problem exists (target: week 1)

- [ ] I have contacted at least 20 photographers (logged in `interview-log.md`)
- [ ] At least 10 replied in some form
- [ ] At least 5 gave me a **specific, dated** example of a slow or missed follow-up
- [ ] At least 3 said something that shows it bothers them (frustration, guilt, losing a couple)
- [ ] I recorded their **exact words** and used them in my outreach copy
- [ ] I know the top 3 channels where their enquiries actually come from

### Level 2 — The offer is understood (target: week 1)

- [ ] At least 10 photographers understood what I sell within one message (no confusion about "leads" or "ads")
- [ ] At least 3 asked to see the sample tracker or report
- [ ] At least 1 asked about price unprompted
- [ ] I answered "are you a photographer?" honestly every time
- [ ] I never claimed to generate leads, and never claimed anyone was losing customers

### Level 3 — Willingness to pay (target: day 7–14)

- [ ] I asked for ₹799 directly, at least 3 times, in plain words (no "if you're interested, let me know")
- [ ] At least 1 person opened the payment link
- [ ] **At least 1 person paid ₹400**
- [ ] Money arrived in a real, verified account (see `07-payments-and-delivery/payment-guide-india.md`)

### Level 4 — Delivery works (target: days 8–14)

- [ ] Client completed the onboarding questionnaire
- [ ] Input format was followed (one clean list came in, not screenshots)
- [ ] Tracker delivered, correct, and the client could open it without help
- [ ] Up to 30 drafts delivered, in their voice, approved by them
- [ ] Client sent **at least one** message themselves using my draft (the real proof)
- [ ] Handover call/voice note done, ≤20 minutes
- [ ] Day-14 check-in scheduled and completed
- [ ] Copies of their data deleted within 30 days, confirmed in writing

### Level 5 — Is this a business? (target: 60–90 days)

- [ ] 3 paid clients delivered with no complaints
- [ ] At least 1 client asked a question *after* handover (= they are still using it)
- [ ] Average delivery time under 6 hours
- [ ] At least 1 written testimonial or honest review with permission to use it
- [ ] At least 1 referral or repeat enquiry from a past client
- [ ] I have a clear answer to "why should the next client pay ₹1,499 instead of ₹799"

## 3. What "success" means for the pilot (short version)

**Success is NOT** a booked wedding for the photographer, more Instagram followers, or a nice-looking PDF.

**Success IS:**
1. A photographer pays ₹799 and gets a tracker they actually open again.
2. They send at least one follow-up they would not otherwise have sent.
3. They can tell you, in one sentence, what to do next for each enquiry on their list.

**The photographer's outcome is theirs to earn.** We improve the process and the clarity. Bookings depend on their price, portfolio, availability, and the couple's choices — none of which we control. Say this out loud during the sale; it prevents disappointment later.

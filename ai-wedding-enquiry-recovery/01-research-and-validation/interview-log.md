## Example of a properly filled row (this is a made-up illustration, not a real interview)

```
DATE:             2026-03-04
NAME / STUDIO:    "R.K." (studio of 1)
CITY:             Indore
CHANNEL:          Instagram DM → moved to WhatsApp
PROFILE FIT:      Solo, ~6 years, est. 15–20 enquiries/month, active weekly
CONVERSATION?:    Yes — 12 min WhatsApp voice call

Q3  Enquiries in last 2 months (their number): "Around 30–35, mostly Instagram DMs"
Q4  How many still open / unresolved: "Honestly no idea. Maybe 10?"
Q6  Where enquiry details live: "WhatsApp chats, some in Instagram, one or two in my Notes app"
Q7  Specific incident: "A July couple enquired, I sent my price list, then I got busy with
    a 3-day wedding and replied 11 days later. They had already booked someone."
Q8  What they do when a couple goes quiet: "Nothing. I feel like I'm begging if I message again."
Q10 Ever paid for leads?: "No, but my friend bought a portal package and got nothing."
Q11 Software in use: "Nothing, just WhatsApp labels"

THEIR WORDS: "I feel like I'm begging if I message again."

SCORE (0–5): 4
OBJECTION RAISED: None yet — asked me to send the sample
NEXT ACTION + DATE: Send sample tracker + Sprint offer on 2026-03-05, follow up 2026-03-08
SAMPLE SENT? (Y/N): Y
```

> **Why this example matters:** the value is in *"I feel like I'm begging if I message again."* That sentence is a message-writing problem and an emotional problem — and it tells you exactly what to lead with when you offer the service.

# Service Agreement Template

> **Read this first:** This is a plain-English service agreement written for a small Indian service provider working as a sole proprietor/individual. It is a good-faith template, **not legal advice**. It is intentionally short so a photographer will actually read and sign it. If your work grows, have a lawyer review it. Do not add clauses you don't understand and cannot explain.

**How to use:** copy into a Google Doc → replace all `[placeholders]` → send to the client → they reply "I agree" by email/WhatsApp, or sign and send back a photo/PDF. **A written "I agree" in the chat is a valid record of acceptance** for a ₹799 service; keep a screenshot.

## 1. Service

The Service Provider will deliver the **7-Day Enquiry Recovery Sprint**, consisting of:

1. **Enquiry Tracker** — a spreadsheet organising up to **15** of the Client's existing enquiries, with status, next action, follow-up due date, priority flags and a dashboard.
2. **Status & Next-Action Framework** — a one-page reference explaining each status.
3. **Personalised Follow-Up Drafts** — up to **30** message drafts, personalised to the Client's enquiries and tone.
4. **7-Day Follow-Up Workflow** — the process for continuing the system after delivery.
5. **Handover Guide** — a short document plus one walkthrough call or voice note (maximum 30 minutes).
6. **Results Summary** — a short factual report prepared after the Day-14 check-in, using only numbers reported by the Client.

Materials are delivered in a Google Drive folder / Google Sheet created under the **Client's** Google account, or shared with the Client's Google account.

## 2. Price and payment

- **Total fee: ₹799 (Rupees Seven Hundred Ninety-Nine only).**
- **Advance: ₹400**, payable before work begins.
- **Balance: ₹399**, payable on handover, before the final documents are released.
- Payment by UPI or UPI payment link only. No GST is charged or payable: the Service Provider's turnover is below the GST registration threshold, and no GSTIN is shown on the invoice.
- **Extensions:** every additional 10 enquiries = **₹399**, payable in advance before that work begins. Extensions are optional and never added automatically.
- **Out-of-scope requests:** quoted separately in writing and paid before the work starts.

## 3. Timeline

- Delivery begins the next working day after **both** (a) the advance is received, and (b) the completed onboarding questionnaire and enquiry list are received.
- The deliverables listed in clause 1 are due within **7 calendar days** of that start date.
- **Client-dependent delays:** the timeline pauses if the Client does not provide the inputs, or does not respond to draft approvals, within 3 days. Delivery resumes when they do. The Client will be informed by message when the timeline pauses.
- If the Service Provider needs more time for any reason within their control, they will inform the Client **before** the deadline and give a new date. Silence is not acceptable to either party.

## 4. What is NOT included

This is a closed scope. The following are explicitly excluded:

- Sending messages to the Client's couples or any third party. **The Client sends every message.**
- Lead generation, advertising, marketing, SEO, or any promise of new enquiries.
- Bulk messaging, WhatsApp automation, broadcast tools, or messaging outside official platform rules.
- Setting up or paying for any CRM or paid software.
- Website, logo, portfolio or social media work.
- Photo or video editing, culling, colour grading, or album design.
- Contracts, invoices or collection of money from the Client's couples.
- Calling or messaging the Client's couples' family members or wedding planners.
- Any guarantee of bookings, replies, conversion rates, or revenue.
- Ongoing management after Day 14 (a separate service, not sold under this agreement).

## 5. Revisions

- **One round of revisions** on the drafts or the tracker is included, free, if requested within **5 days** of delivery.
- Revisions cover tone, length, wording and corrections.
- **Not included:** rewriting for a different brand voice after approval, adding new enquiries after handover, or work caused by inaccurate information supplied by the Client.
- If the Client supplied incorrect information that must be redone, the first correction is free; further ones may be charged at ₹199, agreed in writing beforehand.

## 6. Client responsibilities

The Client will:

1. Pay the advance before work begins.
2. Complete the onboarding questionnaire and provide the enquiry list within **48 hours** of paying the advance.
3. Provide enquiry information in the agreed simple format, using initials or first names only.
4. Respond to draft-approval messages within **48 hours**.
5. **Read every draft and decide whether to send it.** The Client is the sender and takes responsibility for the content of messages they send.
6. Not ask the Service Provider to contact any couple who has asked not to be contacted.
7. Pay the balance on handover.

## 7. Service Provider responsibilities

The Service Provider will:

1. Deliver the deliverables in clause 1 within the timeline in clause 3.
2. Personalise drafts to the Client's actual enquiries and tone.
3. Reply to the Client's questions within 24 hours on working days.
4. Keep the Client's information confidential.
5. Process enquiry information only as instructed by the Client and only for this service.
6. Never contact the Client's couples or any third party on the Client's behalf.
7. Delete all working copies of enquiry data within **30 days** after delivery, and confirm the deletion in writing.
8. Never publish the Client's data or use it as a public case study without the Client's written permission.
9. Not invent results, numbers, testimonials or outcomes in any report.

## 8. Privacy and data handling

- The Client remains responsible for the couple data they hold and for having a lawful basis to contact those couples.
- The Service Provider will ask for the **minimum** information: initials or first names, source, dates, and what was discussed. **No phone numbers, email addresses, identity documents or photographs of couples.**
- Where practical, work happens inside a folder owned by the Client. The Service Provider's access is removed at handover.
- Either party may ask for earlier deletion, and the other will confirm within 24 hours.
- The Service Provider will notify the Client within 72 hours if any unauthorised access to their information is discovered, and will state plainly what happened.

## 9. Refund policy

- The advance is **fully refunded** if the Service Provider fails to deliver the deliverables in clause 1 within 14 days for a reason within the Service Provider's control.
- **No refund** after the tracker and drafts have been delivered, or for a change of mind.
- If the Client does not provide the required inputs within 7 days of paying the advance, the advance is non-refundable (the time slot was reserved). The Service Provider may instead deliver a generic template version if the Client prefers — this will be agreed in writing.

## 10. Limits of the service (what no one can promise)

The Client acknowledges and agrees that:

1. The Service Provider **does not generate leads**, and this agreement does not include marketing of any kind.
2. The Service Provider **does not contact the Client's couples.** All messages are sent by the Client.
3. The Service Provider **makes no guarantee** of bookings, replies, conversions, revenue, or "recovered" value.
4. Whether a couple books depends on the Client's portfolio, pricing, availability, contract terms and the couple's own decision.
5. Outcome figures in the Results Summary are **reported by the Client** and are not independently verified.
6. Any research, examples or general industry information shared by the Service Provider is context, not a promise about the Client's results.

## 11. Intellectual property

- The Client owns the tracker, drafts and documents produced for them, and may use and modify them freely.
- The Service Provider retains the right to reuse generic templates, structures, spreadsheets and methods (without any of the Client's data or identifiable details) in other engagements.

## 12. Confidentiality

Both parties will keep the other's non-public business information confidential and will not share it with third parties, except where required by law. This obligation continues after the engagement ends.

## 13. Communication and termination

- Communication will be by WhatsApp and email, during reasonable hours.
- Either party may end this agreement by written message at any time. If the Client ends it before delivery, work already completed is payable on a reasonable pro-rata basis, and the unused portion of the advance is refunded.
- If either party behaves abusively or dishonestly, the agreement ends immediately.

## 14. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India, and disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at `[your city]`.

## Internal notes for you (delete before sending)

- Keep a copy of every accepted agreement in a folder named `Clients/[client name]/`.
- If the client asks for changes to clause 9 or 10, read them carefully. **Never concede clause 10.**
- If the client asks you to message their couples "just once, from your number" — decline politely. It breaks the whole design (privacy, consent, and your own reputation) and it is not worth ₹799.
- If the client wants to pay everything at the end instead of an advance: acceptable **only** for clients who came through a referral you trust. Otherwise, the advance is what makes the 7-day promise possible.

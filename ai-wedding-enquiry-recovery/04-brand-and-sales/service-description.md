# Concise Service Description

**Use for:** Instagram bio, WhatsApp Business description, website hero, directory listings, email signature. Keep it identical everywhere — consistency is what makes a one-person business look reliable.

---

## The 30-word version (Instagram bio / WhatsApp description)

> Enquiry recovery for wedding photographers. I organise your open enquiries into one tracker and write the follow-ups. 7 days. ₹799. No software to buy.

## The 60-word version (website hero / directory listing)

> **Your enquiries, organised in 7 days.**
> Wedding enquiries arrive in WhatsApp, Instagram, your website and portals — and the follow-ups are the first thing to slip when you're shooting. I take up to 15 of your open enquiries, put them in one tracker with a clear next action each, and write up to 30 personalised follow-up messages in your own tone. ₹799. You send every message.

## The 100-word version (proposal intro / LinkedIn-style summary)

> Most wedding photographers in India don't lose enquiries because they don't care — they lose them because a couple enquires on Instagram on a shoot day, the reply happens four days later, the quote goes out, and then life happens. Three weeks later nobody knows who is still waiting for an answer.
>
> The 7-Day Enquiry Recovery Sprint fixes that once, properly: up to 15 existing enquiries organised into a single tracker with statuses, next actions and due dates; up to 30 follow-up drafts written in your own voice; and a simple process that takes five minutes a day to keep going. ₹799 flat, 7 days, no software subscription. I draft — you send. I never contact your couples.

## What I say in one sentence (out loud, on a call)

> *"I take the enquiries you already have, put them in one place, tell you what to do next for each one, and write the follow-up messages so you don't have to think about what to type."*

## What I never say

- "I'll get you more bookings."
- "I'll recover your lost revenue."
- "I'll message your clients for you."
- "Guaranteed results."

## Three sentences that handle 90% of first questions

| They ask | You answer |
|---|---|
| "Are you a photographer?" | "No. I'm the person who organises the follow-up work. You stay the photographer." |
| "Do I need to buy any software?" | "No. It runs on Google Sheets and your existing WhatsApp. Nothing to subscribe to." |
| "Will you message my clients?" | "Never. I write the drafts, you read them and send them from your own number." |

## Social proof policy (until you actually have proof)

You have no clients yet. **Do not fake any.** Use these honest substitutes:

1. **Show the work, not the claims.** "Here's a sample tracker with 10 fictional enquiries — see exactly how it works." (Use `03-demo-materials/`.)
2. **State the pilot openly.** "I'm building this for Indian wedding photographers. You'd be one of my first clients, which is why it's ₹799."
3. **Show the thinking.** Post a short insight from your research: *"Most photographers I've spoken to say enquiries arrive on 4 different apps. Where do yours land?"* This is a question, not a claim — it starts conversations.
4. **After your first client, ask for one honest line.** Even *"the tracker made my week easier"* is usable **with written permission**, attributed honestly ("feedback from a photographer in Indore, used with permission").

**Never:** stock testimonials, invented client names, invented booking numbers, "as seen on" logos, or a fake case study.

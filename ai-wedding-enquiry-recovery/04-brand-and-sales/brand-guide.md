# Brand Guide

Everything here is a **starting concept**. Nothing here is registered, trademarked or legally cleared. Check availability before you print anything or buy a domain.

---

## 1. Name

**Business name (working):** **AI Wedding Enquiry Recovery**

**Why it works:** it says exactly what it does, to exactly whom, and it is searchable. It is a working name, not a brand with equity yet.

### Options if you want something warmer or shorter later

| Name | Tagline | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **Enquiry Desk** | *Every enquiry answered.* | Calm, operational, easy to say on a call |
| **Follow-Up Studio** | *The follow-up, handled.* | Service-first, less "AI" |
| **Vivah Desk** | *Har enquiry, jawab ke saath.* | Indian, warm, memorable (verify no conflict before use) |
| **Wedding Enquiry Recovery** | *Turn quiet enquiries into real conversations.* | Descriptive, keyword-friendly for search |

**Recommendation for the 7-day launch:** keep **AI Wedding Enquiry Recovery** on the website title and in your email subject lines (it is descriptive and searchable), but **use "Enquiry Desk" or "7-Day Enquiry Recovery Sprint"** when you speak to photographers, because "AI" can trigger skepticism. You can always rename after your first three clients.

**One rule:** if you rename, rename **everywhere** the same day — website, tracker footer, agreement, invoice, email signature. Inconsistent names read as fake.

## 2. Tagline options

Pick **one** and use it everywhere.

1. *Every enquiry answered. Every follow-up done.* ← recommended
2. *Turn your quiet enquiries into real conversations.*
3. *You shoot the weddings. I'll handle the follow-ups.*
4. *7 days. One organised enquiry list. Zero software to buy.*
5. *The follow-up, handled.*

**Never use:** "Never lose a booking again", "Guaranteed bookings", "Recover lost revenue" — these are promises you cannot keep.

## 3. Visual identity

### 3.1 Colours

| Role | Hex | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Primary — Deep Rose | `#7C2D4A` | Headings, buttons, logo mark, email headers |
| Primary light | `#F3E8EE` | Table headers, section tints |
| Accent — Warm Gold | `#C99A4B` | Small highlights, dividers, "Sprint" labels |
| Ink — Charcoal | `#1F2A37` | Body text |
| Muted grey | `#6B7280` | Secondary text, captions |
| Background — Cream | `#FBF7F4` | Page background, documents |
| Success green | `#DCF5E3` / text `#12703A` | Booked, completed |
| Warning amber | `#FDF3D7` / text `#9A3412` | Due soon, upcoming wedding |
| Danger red | `#FDE2E1` / text `#B91C1C` | Overdue, do-not-contact |
| Neutral grey | `#F1F2F4` | Closed, dormant, disabled |

**Why these colours:** deep rose reads as wedding-adjacent and premium without being bridal-cliché pink; gold adds occasion; the functional red/amber/green are the same colours used in the tracker, so the documents and the spreadsheets feel like one product.

**Rule:** never use colour alone to carry meaning. In the tracker, "OVERDUE" is written in words *and* shaded red — that matters for readability and for accessibility.

### 3.2 Typography (all free)

| Use | Font | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Headings | **Playfair Display** (Google Fonts, free) | Georgia, serif |
| Body and UI | **Inter** (Google Fonts, free) | system-ui, Segoe UI, Arial |

If you cannot load web fonts (offline, slow connection), Georgia + Arial are already on every device and look perfectly professional. Do not pay for fonts at this stage.

### 3.3 Logo concept

Three files are provided in this folder:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | Full lockup (mark + wordmark) for the website header, proposal, agreement, and invoice |
| `logo-mark.svg` | Square mark only — Instagram profile picture, favicon, WhatsApp Business photo |
| `logo-mark-256.png` | *(generate from the SVG if you need a raster version for a platform that rejects SVG)* |

**The idea behind the mark:** a rounded speech bubble (the enquiry) inside a thin ring (a wedding band, and also a lens). The small gold dot is the "unread notification" — the enquiry that was about to be forgotten. It is deliberately simple enough to read at 32 × 32 pixels.

**Design rules:**
- Clear space around the logo = the height of the ring's stroke, minimum.
- Minimum size: 24 px for the mark, 120 px wide for the full lockup.
- On dark backgrounds use the cream wordmark; on cream backgrounds use the rose wordmark.
- Never stretch, rotate, add drop shadows, or re-colour it outside this palette.

**If you want a different logo:** search Google Fonts for an elegant serif, type the business name, and pair it with a simple line-art icon you draw in Canva (free) or Google Drawings (free). A clean wordmark alone is better than a busy logo. **Do not spend money on a designer before your first client.**

## 4. Voice and tone

**Write like a helpful professional, not a salesperson.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your enquiries, organised." | "Revolutionise your booking pipeline!" |
| "I write the drafts. You send them." | "We'll automate your client communication." |
| "I'm not a photographer." | Pretend to be an insider |
| "₹799, flat." | "Special limited-time offer!!" |
| "Here's a sample so you can judge." | "Trust me, it works." |
| "If it's not useful, tell me to stop." | "Last chance to book this slot!" |

**The five words to use often:** *organise, next action, follow-up, draft, handover.*
**The five words to avoid:** *guarantee, ROI, boost, hack, automate* (unless it is genuinely automated — and it is not).

### Language
- Write in **simple English**. Short sentences. No jargon — no "funnel optimisation", no "lead nurturing".
- Hinglish is fine in WhatsApp messages to Indian photographers when they use it first. Match their register; don't fake it.
- Indian spelling and formatting: ₹799, 12 October 2026, WhatsApp (not Whatsapp).

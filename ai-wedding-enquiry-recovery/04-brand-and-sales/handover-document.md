## 1. The one habit that makes this work

**Every morning, open the tracker and filter `Priority` for `P1 - today`. Send those messages. Update the row. Done.**

That's it. If you do only this, the system works. Everything below is detail for the days you want it.

Takes: **5 minutes a day.**

---

## 2. The four things you type

For each enquiry, you only ever change four cells:

| Column | What to put | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **G — Enquiry Status** | Where it stands (dropdown) | `Quote sent` |
| **H — Last Contact Date** | The date you last messaged them | `12-Oct-2026` |
| **I — Next Action** | The next single thing to do (dropdown) | `Gentle nudge (no price talk)` |
| **K — Follow-Up Status** | Done / due / late (dropdown) | `Due` |

Everything else — days since contact, priority, the red OVERDUE flag — **calculates itself.** Don't type in columns N, O or P.

**When something final happens**, set `L — Outcome` (`Booked`, `Closed - chose another vendor`, etc.). That's the data that tells you where you actually win and lose.

---

## 3. Your daily 5 minutes (step by step)

1. Open the tracker.
2. Click the filter arrow on column **O (Priority)** → tick only **`P1 - today`**.
3. You will see 0–4 rows. These are the only ones that matter today.
4. For each: open your drafts document, copy the matching message, read it, edit if needed, send it.
5. Come back and update **Last Contact Date** (today) and **Follow-Up Status** (`Completed`).
6. Set the next **Follow-Up Due Date** (usually +3 days, or +7 days for the second nudge).
7. Clear the filter. Close it.

**Then:** click the filter on column **G (Enquiry Status)** → `New enquiry`. If anyone is there, reply to them today. New enquiries are the most valuable messages you will ever send.

---

## 4. Your weekly 20-minute blocks

Put these in your calendar as recurring reminders:

**Wednesday (20 min) — Follow-up block**
Filter `Follow-Up Status` → `Due` or `Overdue`. Send the matching drafts. Update the rows.

**Friday (20 min) — Review**
Open the **Dashboard** tab. Look at four numbers:
- Open enquiries
- Overdue follow-ups *(aim for zero)*
- New enquiries unanswered *(aim for zero)*
- Weddings in the next 30 days *(your near-term focus)*

Then decide: is anyone stuck for no good reason? Fix it now.

**Sunday night (30 min, optional but recommended) — Plan the week**
Look at the weddings happening in the next 30 days. Message those couples with a confirmation and any logistics question. This is where future referrals come from.

---

## 5. When a new enquiry arrives

1. Add a row the **same day**. (A tracker that is two days stale is a tracker you'll stop trusting.)
2. Fill: ID, today's date, initials, service, event date, source.
3. Set Status = `New enquiry`, Next Action = `Reply with welcome message + 3 questions`, Follow-Up Due = **today**.
4. Reply **within 24 hours**, ideally within an hour or two. Speed matters more than perfect wording.
5. Update the row after you send.

**Add it on your phone if that's where you are** — the Google Sheets app works fine for adding a row. Don't wait until you're at the laptop.

---

## 6. The stop rule (this protects your reputation)

**Maximum 3 follow-ups after your first reply, per enquiry. Then stop.**

Stop **immediately and permanently** when someone:
- says they're not interested,
- says they've booked someone else,
- asks you to stop messaging,
- or blocks you.

When that happens: set Status = `Closed - not proceeding` (or `Dormant - no reply`) and **never message them again** — not in six months, not ever. If they come back later, welcome them warmly.

**Why this matters commercially:** in a referral-driven business, the graceful exit *is* marketing. A couple who felt respected after saying no is a couple who recommends you.

## 7. Copy-paste routine for your drafts

1. Open the drafts document.
2. Find the block with the enquiry ID you're working on.
3. Copy the WhatsApp version (short) or the email version (longer) — whichever fits the channel they used.
4. Read it once and change anything that doesn't sound like you.
5. Send it from **your** number.
6. Update the tracker row.

**Never send a draft without reading it.** You are the sender; you own the message.

## 9. If you get stuck

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You don't know what status to choose | Open the **Status Guide** tab. If it's still unclear, pick the closest one and move on — a slightly wrong status is better than a stale tracker |
| A formula looks broken | Don't fight it. Just type the value in the cell. It's your sheet — the formula is a convenience, not a law |
| You added rows and lost the dropdowns | Select the column → Data → Data validation → extend the range to row 500 |
| You want to add your own status | Fine. But also update any Dashboard count you care about |
| You're overwhelmed | Do only this: filter `Priority = P1 - today`, send those, close the sheet |
| Something in the drafts feels wrong | Message me. Revisions are free within 5 days of delivery |

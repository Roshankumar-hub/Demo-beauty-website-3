# Client Onboarding Questionnaire

**How to use:** create this as a **Google Form** (free, 10 minutes) and send the link the moment the advance arrives. Or copy it into a Doc/WhatsApp if the photographer prefers. Their answers are everything you need to start — if you have to chase more than twice, the sprint will slip, so make it easy.

> **Privacy reminder at the top of the form (paste it in):**
> *"Please do not send couples' phone numbers, email addresses, or any documents. Initials or first names are enough. I only need the details below."*

---

## Section 1 — About your business (2 minutes)

1. **Studio / photographer name** (exactly as it should appear on documents)
2. **City / base location**
3. **Your WhatsApp number** (for me to reach *you* — never a couple's)
4. **Your email**
5. **Years in business** and roughly **how many weddings per year**
6. **Do you work solo or with a team?** If a team, who replies to enquiries besides you?

## Section 2 — Your enquiry flow (2 minutes)

7. **Where do your enquiries come from?** (tick all) — Instagram DM / Instagram comments / WhatsApp / website form / referral / wedding portal / Google / other
8. **Roughly how many enquiries a month** do you get in peak season?
9. **Where do you currently keep the details** of an enquiry? (WhatsApp chats / Instagram / email inbox / a notebook / a spreadsheet / nothing)
10. **Who replies** to enquiries — you, or someone else?
11. **What usually happens after you send your price list?** (Do you follow up? How many times? What do you write?)

## Section 3 — Your packages and pricing (3 minutes)

12. **Your package names and prices** (or a price range per wedding scale). You can also just attach your price list.
13. **What is included** in the most common package? (hours/days of coverage, number of photographers, photos, album, film, travel)
14. **What is NOT included** and is chargeable separately? (travel, stay, extra hours, drone, album, extra day)
15. **Booking terms:** what percentage blocks the date, and when is the balance due?
16. **Delivery timelines:** photos in how many days/weeks? Film? Album?
17. **Any dates you are already booked for** (so I never write a message that contradicts your availability)
18. **Do you offer** a discount, a weekday rate, or a package adjustment when someone's budget is short? (If yes, what are you comfortable with; if no, say no — I will not invent one.)

## Section 4 — Your voice (3 minutes — this is the most important section)

19. **Paste 3–5 messages you have actually sent to couples** (WhatsApp or Instagram). Remove names and numbers first. These teach me how you write.
20. **Your tone:** formal / warm / casual / Hinglish-friendly — pick one, or describe it in your own words.
21. **How do you usually sign off?** (e.g. "— Raj", "Regards, Studio X", just your first name)
22. **Words or phrases you hate** and would never send
23. **Do you use emojis?** never / rarely / often — and which ones?
24. **How do you like to talk about price?** directly in the first message / after a call / only in the price list

## Section 5 — The enquiries (5 minutes)

25. **Paste your open enquiries** into the format below — up to 15. Use **initials or first names only**.

| # | Initials | Source (IG/WhatsApp/etc.) | Enquired on (date) | Event date | Service they asked about | What was discussed/sent so far | Last contact date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | | | | |
| 2 | | | | | | | |
| … | | | | | | | |

**Or, if typing it out is slow:** send me 5–10 screenshots of the chats and I'll do the extraction and send you the finished list back to verify. *(Screenshots are less private — delete them from your side after, and I will delete mine within 7 days.)*

26. **Are any of these couples people you do NOT want contacted again?** List them so I exclude them completely. *(Important — this is how we respect consent.)*
27. **Which enquiries feel most important to you right now?** (so I prioritise them)

## Section 6 — Practical bits (1 minute)

28. **What should I call the tracker file?** (default: `[Studio Name] — Enquiry Tracker`)
29. **Google account email** to share the tracker with (if different from above)
30. **Best time to reach you** for the handover call
31. **Anything else I should know?** (a busy season coming, a family situation, a date you're anxious about — anything that helps me write better messages)

## Consent confirmation (required before I start)

> *"I confirm that: (a) these enquiries came to me directly and the couples contacted me first; (b) I am permitted to review and contact them; (c) I authorise [Your name] to process this enquiry information only to organise my enquiries and prepare message drafts for me, and to delete any working copies within 30 days after delivery; (d) I understand that I will send every message myself."*

## For you (the service provider) — intake checklist

- [ ] Advance ₹400 received and verified
- [ ] All 31 answers received
- [ ] At least 3 sample messages provided (if not, ask once more — the drafts will sound generic without them)
- [ ] Enquiry list received, with 15 or fewer rows
- [ ] Consent box ticked
- [ ] Any "do not contact" couples flagged and excluded
- [ ] Price list understood (no contradictions in drafts)
- [ ] Dates of unavailability recorded
- [ ] Tracker file created **in the client's Google account**, shared to their email
- [ ] Delivery start date confirmed back to the client in one message

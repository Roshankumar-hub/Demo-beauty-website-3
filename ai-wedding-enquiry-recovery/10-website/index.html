The part that slips

Nobody forgets on purpose

A couple enquires on a shoot day. The reply goes out three days later. The quote goes out the week after. Then a three-day wedding happens, and by the time you look up, you genuinely don't know who is still waiting for an answer.

It isn't a discipline problem. It's that the details live in five different apps and no single place shows you the next move.

What I found talking to studios

Across the studios I've spoken to, the same pattern appears:

  • Enquiries scattered across Instagram, WhatsApp, a website form and sometimes a portal
  • Quotes sent, then silence — and no follow-up because "it feels like begging"
  • No single number for how many enquiries are actually open right now
  • Plenty of willingness to spend money on leads, very little spent on converting the ones already coming in

These are patterns from conversations, not a survey. Your situation may be different — that's worth finding out before you pay anything.

Questions

Straight answers

Are you a photographer?

No, and I won't pretend to be. You're the photographer — I'm the person who sorts the enquiries, decides the next move, and writes the messages. Every draft is something you approve or throw away.

Will you contact my couples?

Never. Not once, on any channel. I write the drafts; you read them and send them from your own number. That keeps it your relationship, and it keeps me out of platform and privacy rules around messaging your clients.

Do I need to buy any software?

No. It runs on a free Google Sheet and the WhatsApp and Instagram you already use. Nothing to subscribe to, nothing to install.

How much time do I need to give you?

About 10 minutes for the form, 20 minutes to look at the drafts twice, and 15 minutes for the handover call. Then about 5 minutes a day to run it.

What exactly do you need from me?

Up to 15 open enquiries with initials or first names only — no phone numbers, no emails. Your package prices. And 3–5 messages you've already sent to couples, so the drafts sound like you rather than like a robot.

Will this get me more bookings?

I don't know, and I won't claim it will. What I can promise is specific: up to 15 enquiries organised with a next action for each, up to 30 personalised drafts, delivered in 7 days. Whether a couple books depends on your portfolio, your pricing, your availability and their decision — that part is yours, and you're better at it than I am.

I already use a CRM. Do I need this?

Maybe not. One honest test: open your CRM and check whether you know the next action for the five longest-waiting enquiries. If the answer is yes, you don't need me and I'll tell you so. If the CRM has the data but the follow-ups still aren't happening, that's the problem I fix — alongside whatever you already pay for.

Why is it only ₹799?

It's a pilot price. You'd be one of my first clients while I build this, so I'm pricing for honest feedback and a case study as much as for money. Once I have a few delivered clients, the price goes up. If it seems too cheap to be good, ask for the sample and judge the work instead of the price.

What if I'm unhappy?

Tell me. One revision round is included free within 5 days of delivery. If I fail to deliver within 14 days for any reason on my side, your advance is refunded in full — it's written into the agreement you'll get before paying.

What about my clients' privacy?

Your couples' data stays under your control. I ask for initials only, I work inside a folder you own, I never contact your clients, and I delete my working copies within 30 days and confirm it in writing. If you want your data deleted sooner, one message is enough.

Is this a registered company?

No. I'm an individual running a small service, below the GST registration threshold, and I invoice accordingly — a plain invoice, no GST charged. I'd rather tell you plainly than pretend to be bigger than I am.

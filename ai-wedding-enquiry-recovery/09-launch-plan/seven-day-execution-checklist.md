# Seven-Day Execution Checklist (one page, tick every box)

**Objective: attempt to get one paying customer in 7 days. It is a target, not a promise.**
**Budget: ₹0.** **Time: 2–3.5 hours a day.**

Tick each box only when it is genuinely done. If something is unticked at the end of a day, carry it into the next morning — **but never at the cost of the day's outreach.**

---

## ☐ DAY 1 — Understand and set up (2.5 h)

- [ ] Read `market-research.md` and `idea-assessment.md`
- [ ] Read `service-offer.md`; I accept 15 enquiries / 30 drafts / ₹799
- [ ] I can say my offer in one sentence, out loud, without reading
- [ ] I can explain what is NOT included (no leads · no messaging their clients · no guarantee)
- [ ] Read the 12 discovery questions; rewrote 3 in my own words
- [ ] Business Gmail account created
- [ ] 2-step verification switched **ON**
- [ ] UPI test payment of **₹1 verified in my own app**
- [ ] Payment ledger sheet created
- [ ] Read the decision rules and accepted them in advance
- [ ] **Spent nothing**

## ☐ DAY 2 — Build the demo (3 h)

- [ ] Sample tracker opens in Google Sheets (or Excel) with working dropdowns and colours
- [ ] Shareable **view** link for the sample created and tested in an incognito window
- [ ] Sample opens correctly on my phone
- [ ] Rewrote 2 message templates as real examples in my own words
- [ ] Can explain the daily routine in 30 seconds
- [ ] Read the sample report and understood why it avoids claiming results
- [ ] **Dry run done:** 8 fictional enquiries fully processed; time recorded: ____ h
- [ ] If the dry run took over 5 hours → pilot capped at 12 enquiries for client #1
- [ ] I could rebuild the tracker from scratch if I had to

## ☐ DAY 3 — Find prospects (3 h)

- [ ] Two focus cities chosen
- [ ] **30 prospects** researched and entered in `prospect-tracker.xlsx`
- [ ] Each scored using the playbook; **20 qualified** at 4+
- [ ] Every qualified prospect has a **specific** observation (never "love your work")
- [ ] Every one has a real, public contact route
- [ ] Zero guessed, scraped or private contact details
- [ ] Cold DM + email scripts adapted into my own words and saved on my phone

## ☐ DAY 4 — Start outreach (2.5 h)

- [ ] **10 first messages sent** (aim 6 form/email, 4 DM)
- [ ] Each message: identifies me · specific observation · one ask · opt-out line
- [ ] Tracker updated with dates and channels
- [ ] 15 more prospects researched for the queue
- [ ] I did not send identical text to ten people

## ☐ DAY 5 — Conversations and demos (3 h)

- [ ] 10 more first messages sent (**20 cumulative**)
- [ ] Follow-up #1 sent to anyone silent since Day 3–4
- [ ] Every reply answered the same day
- [ ] Discovery questions asked (max 3) before any pitch
- [ ] Samples sent to everyone interested
- [ ] Read `objection-handling.md`; rehearsed my top 5 objections out loud
- [ ] Anyone who declined marked **`DO NOT CONTACT`**

## ☐ DAY 6 — Make the offer (2.5 h)

- [ ] 10 more first messages sent (**30 cumulative**) and all follow-ups sent
- [ ] **3+ explicit offers made** with the payment link (not "let me know if interested")
- [ ] The scope confirmation message written and ready to send
- [ ] I did not discount, exaggerate, or invent urgency
- [ ] Anyone who clearly doesn't need it was told honestly and left alone

## ☐ DAY 7 — Payment and delivery (3 h)

- [ ] Checked my payment app first thing
- [ ] **If paid:** invoice sent · onboarding form sent · scope confirmed · delivery dates confirmed · tracker folder created
- [ ] **If not paid:** one reminder sent to anyone who said yes · 10 more first messages sent
- [ ] Payment verified in **my own app**, never from a screenshot alone
- [ ] Weekly summary filled in with real numbers
- [ ] Decision written down: continue / change offer / change audience

## Non-negotiables (never break these)

- [ ] I never claimed to generate leads, bookings, or revenue
- [ ] I never told anyone they were losing customers unless they said so first
- [ ] I never invented a testimonial, a client, or a result
- [ ] I never contacted anyone who asked me to stop
- [ ] I never sent more than one follow-up to a silent prospect
- [ ] I never bulk-sent, scraped, or bought a list
- [ ] I never sent messages to anyone's clients on their behalf
- [ ] I never spent money
- [ ] I told the truth about my experience level

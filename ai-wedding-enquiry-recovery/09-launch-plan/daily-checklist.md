# Daily Checklist — print this and tick it every day

**The one rule of this page: outreach first. Admin second. Every day.**

---

## Every day (about 90 minutes)

### Morning — 30 minutes: prospects
- [ ] Research **10–20** new prospects (only those scoring 4+)
- [ ] Write a **specific observation** for each (the work, not the person)
- [ ] Log them in `05-prospecting/prospect-tracker.xlsx`

### Midday — 40 minutes: messages
- [ ] Send **10 first messages** (spread across channels: website form / email / business DM)
- [ ] Send **all due follow-ups** (only those whose 4-day wait has passed)
- [ ] Every message ends with a way out: *"If this isn't relevant, tell me and I won't message again."*

### Evening — 20 minutes: pipeline
- [ ] Reply to **every** human who replied (same day, within a few hours if possible)
- [ ] Send samples to anyone interested
- [ ] Update the tracker: outreach date, response, interest level, next action + date
- [ ] Mark anyone who said no or asked you to stop as **`DO NOT CONTACT`**

| Metric | Target | My number |
|---|---|---|

| Follow-ups sent | 20–30 | ____ |

| Real conversations (2+ exchanges) | 5+ | ____ |
| Samples sent | 3+ | ____ |
| **Explicit offers made** | **3+** | ____ |
| Payments received | 1 (the target) | ____ |

**Judge the week on the first six rows, not the last one.** The last row is an outcome; the others are your work.

---

## Daily self-check (30 seconds)

- [ ] Did I send messages **before** doing admin today?
- [ ] Was every first line personalised?
- [ ] Did I ask for the money **explicitly** from anyone ready to hear it?
- [ ] Did I tell the truth about every single thing?
- [ ] Did I respect every "no" and every opt-out?
- [ ] Did I log everything in the same day I did it?

If you ticked all six: **you had a good day, regardless of what anyone replied.**

---

## Red flags and what to do

| Signal | What it means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| I haven't messaged anyone for 2 days | The real risk to this business | Send five messages now. Not perfect ones. Five |
| 40 messages, zero replies | Channel or first line problem | Switch channels. Lead with a **question**, not a description |
| Lots of "nice idea" replies, no offers | I'm not asking | Use Message 5. "Shall we do it? Here's the link." |
| Everyone asks about price, nobody buys | Trust gap or wrong audience | Lead with the sample earlier; check they're the right segment |
| Someone said yes and I feel scared | Good — that's the job | Send the onboarding form and invoice within the hour |
| I'm tempted to discount | Panic, not strategy | Offer the smaller ₹399 version instead. Never discount the main offer |
| I'm tempted to skip logging | It will cost you a real follow-up tomorrow | Two minutes. Log it now |

---

## End of week — 25 minutes

- [ ] Total contacts, replies, conversations, samples, offers, payments — real numbers only
- [ ] Most common objection heard → what I'll say differently next week
- [ ] Which channel produced the best conversations → do more of it
- [ ] Exact words prospects used about the problem → put them into my outreach copy
- [ ] One task I avoided this week because it was uncomfortable → **that is tomorrow's first task**
- [ ] Decision, written down: continue as-is / change the offer / change the audience (use `01-research-and-validation/validation-experiment.md` §7)

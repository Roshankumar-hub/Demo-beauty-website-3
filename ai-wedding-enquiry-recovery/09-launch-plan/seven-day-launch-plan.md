# Seven-Day Launch Plan

**The objective:** attempt to obtain **one paying customer** within seven days.
**This is a target, not a promise.** Nobody can guarantee a customer, a booking, or a revenue figure. What you control is the number of conversations you start and the quality of what you show people.

**Daily time budget:** 2–3.5 hours. Total: about 18–20 hours across the week.
**Budget:** ₹0.
**Tools:** your phone, a Google account, a laptop or desktop (borrow one if needed), the files in this project.

> **How to use this plan:** each day has tasks, an output, a checklist, and a "if it doesn't work" fallback. Do the checklist honestly. If a day slips, do not skip the next day's outreach to catch up on admin — outreach is the part that compounds.

## DAY 1 — Evaluate the problem, finalise the offer, prepare discovery questions
**Time: 2.5 hours**

| # | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read `01-research-and-validation/market-research.md` and `idea-assessment.md`. Understand what is proven and what is only assumed | 40 min |
| 2 | Read `02-offer/service-offer.md`. Decide if you accept the scope (15 enquiries / 30 drafts / ₹799). Write your own price and scope in one sentence, in your words | 20 min |
| 3 | Read `01-research-and-validation/validation-experiment.md` including the 12 discovery questions. Rewrite 3 of them so they sound like you | 30 min |
| 4 | Set up your business basics: separate Gmail account, 2-step verification ON, UPI test payment of ₹1 verified, payment ledger sheet created (see `07-payments-and-delivery/payment-guide-india.md` §5) | 40 min |
| 5 | Open the sample tracker (`03-demo-materials/enquiry-tracker/AWER-Enquiry-Tracker-SAMPLE.xlsx`) and the sample report. Look at them as a photographer would. Write down the *first three questions* a photographer would ask you | 20 min |

**Output:** a one-sentence offer you can say out loud without reading; a verified payment method; a working Google account.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] I can say my offer in one clean sentence
- [ ] I can explain what is NOT included (no leads, no messaging their clients, no guarantee)
- [ ] My UPI test payment actually arrived — verified in the app, not assumed
- [ ] Two-factor authentication is ON
- [ ] I have read the decision rules and I accept them in advance
- [ ] I have not spent any money

**If it doesn't work:** if you can't set up payment today (bank issues, KYC delay), **do not delay outreach** — prospects still take a few days to reply. Keep going and fix payment by Day 5.

## DAY 2 — Build your demo materials and rehearsed answers
**Time: 3 hours**

| # | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the tracker in Google Sheets (upload the XLSX or import the CSV — see `03-demo-materials/enquiry-tracker/google-sheets-setup-guide.md`). Confirm the dropdowns, colours and formulas work | 40 min |
| 2 | Read `03-demo-materials/message-templates/client-message-templates.md`. Rewrite **two** templates as a real example for a fictional enquiry, in your own words | 40 min |
| 3 | Read `03-demo-materials/follow-up-workflow.md`. Be able to explain the 5-minute daily routine in 30 seconds | 20 min |
| 4 | Read `03-demo-materials/sample-client-report.md`. Notice how carefully it avoids claiming results — you'll need that discipline when a real client asks "how much did I recover?" | 20 min |
| 5 | **Dry run (the important one):** invent 8 fictional enquiries and do the full process yourself — sort, classify, write 12 drafts. Time it | 60 min |

**Output:** a working sample in Google Sheets; a shareable link; a realistic sense of how long delivery takes you.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Sample tracker opens and works on my phone
- [ ] I have a shareable "view" link for the sample (anyone with the link can **view** — the sample is fictional so this is safe)
- [ ] My dry run took under __ hours; if it took more than 5 hours for 8 enquiries, I will cap the pilot at 12 enquiries for my first client
- [ ] I can explain the daily routine in 30 seconds without notes

**If it doesn't work:** if the spreadsheet is confusing, simplify it — delete columns you don't understand. **A 6-column tracker you can explain beats a 16-column one you can't.** Never show a client something you couldn't rebuild from scratch.

## DAY 3 — Find prospects and build a small, relevant list
**Time: 3 hours**

| # | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read `05-prospecting/playbook.md`. Pick **two cities** you'll focus on | 15 min |
| 2 | Research 30 prospects using Google Maps (15), Instagram hashtags (10) and search engines (5). Fill `05-prospecting/prospect-tracker.xlsx` | 90 min |
| 3 | Score each one from the playbook. Keep only those scoring 4+ | 20 min |
| 4 | Write a **personalised observation** for each of the top 20 (90 seconds each — the work, not the person) | 30 min |
| 5 | Read `05-prospecting/outreach-scripts.md` and adapt the first DM + first email into your own words. Save them as phone notes for speed | 25 min |

**Output:** 30 researched prospects, 20 qualified, each with a specific observation and a chosen channel.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] 30 prospects recorded
- [ ] 20 qualified (scored 4+)
- [ ] Every one of the top 20 has a **specific** observation — not "love your work"
- [ ] Each has a real, public contact route (form / published email / business DM)
- [ ] Zero contact details were guessed, scraped, or taken from a private context
- [ ] I have adapted cold scripts into my own words

**If it doesn't work:** if you can't find 30 in two cities, widen to four cities. If a city yields nothing, it may not have independent photographers — drop it and pick another. **Don't slow down to perfect the list.** 20 good prospects is enough to start Day 4.

## DAY 4 — Begin personalised outreach
**Time: 2.5 hours**

| # | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send **10 first messages** — different channels (aim for 6 website-form/email, 4 Instagram DM). Each opens with that specific observation and ends with the opt-out line | 90 min |
| 2 | Log every send: date, channel, status `Contacted` in the prospect tracker | 20 min |
| 3 | Research 15 more prospects and qualify them (so Day 5 and 6 have a queue) | 40 min |

**Output:** 10 real, personalised messages sent. This is the day that decides everything.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] 10 messages sent (not drafted, not scheduled — **sent**)
- [ ] Each message: identifies me · one specific observation · one clear ask · how to make me stop
- [ ] Max one message per person, one channel
- [ ] Tracker updated the same day
- [ ] I did **not** send the same text to ten people
- [ ] I have not claimed anyone is losing customers

**If it doesn't work:**
- **If you feel too nervous to send:** send three. Then three more. Nervousness is normal; it fades after about twenty messages.
- **If you have no time today:** send **five**. Five is not ten, but it is not zero, and tomorrow you send fifteen.
- **If someone replies rudely:** it is not about you — photographers are pitched constantly. Mark them `DO NOT CONTACT` and move on.

## DAY 5 — Talk to interested prospects, then demonstrate
**Time: 3 hours**

| # | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send **10 more first messages** (you have 15 queued from Day 4) | 60 min |
| 2 | Send **follow-up #1** to anyone from Day 3–4 who hasn't replied after 4 days. Message 7 in `05-prospecting/outreach-scripts.md` | 20 min |
| 3 | Reply to every human who responded — **within a few hours**. Use the message library in `06-sales-system/sales-messages.md` | 45 min |
| 4 | For anyone who replies with interest: run the discovery questions (3 questions max in chat), then send the sample link | 45 min |
| 5 | Read `06-sales-system/objection-handling.md`. Pick the five objections you're most likely to hear and rehearse your answers out loud | 25 min |

**Output:** 20+ messages sent cumulatively; at least 1–3 real conversations; samples sent to anyone interested.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] 20 first messages sent cumulatively
- [ ] Every reply answered the same day
- [ ] Anyone interested has received the sample links
- [ ] I asked the 3 discovery questions before pitching
- [ ] At least one person knows exactly what I do and what it costs
- [ ] I marked anyone who said no as `DO NOT CONTACT`

**If it doesn't work:**
- **Zero replies after 20 messages?** Change the channel, not the message: if you were DMing, switch to website forms and emails (photographers say those get read).
- **Replies but no interest?** You're probably describing the service before asking about their problem. Lead with the question: *"Where do your enquiries live — DMs or WhatsApp?"*
- **Someone says "I don't have this problem"?** Believe them, thank them, and ask if they know one photographer who might — referrals from sceptics convert well.

## DAY 6 — Offer the paid pilot to qualified prospects
**Time: 2.5 hours**

| # | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send **10 more first messages** and **all due follow-ups** | 60 min |
| 2 | To every prospect who has seen the sample or shown interest: **make the offer explicitly** — Message 5, with the payment link. No "let me know if you're interested" | 45 min |
| 3 | For anyone hesitating: send the discovery questions (Message 6) and answer objections honestly. **If someone clearly doesn't need it, say so and stop** | 30 min |
| 4 | Send the scope confirmation message (`07-payments-and-delivery/payment-guide-india.md` §6) to anyone who says yes | 15 min |

**Output:** at least **3 explicit offers made** with a payment link.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] At least 3 people have been asked to pay ₹799 in plain words
- [ ] Every offer included: what's included, what isn't, the price, the split, the timeline
- [ ] I sent the payment link, not just the price
- [ ] I did not discount, exaggerate, or invent urgency
- [ ] Anyone who said no is marked `DO NOT CONTACT`
- [ ] Total messages sent across the week: 30+

**If it doesn't work:**
- **Nobody will say yes at ₹799?** Ask one honest question: *"What would make this worth it for you?"* Their answer tells you whether to change the scope, the price, or the audience. Do not guess.
- **Everyone says "maybe later"?** That is usually a polite no. Offer the **free 15-minute Enquiry Health Check** instead — the smallest possible first step.
- **Three people said "I'd pay if you also did X"?** If all three say the same X, the market just told you what to build. Write it down and adjust the offer for the next ten prospects.

## DAY 7 — Secure the first payment, begin delivery
**Time: 3 hours**

| # | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check your payment app **first thing**. If ₹400 has arrived: celebrate properly for two minutes, then start | 15 min |
| 2 | Send the invoice, the onboarding questionnaire and the scope confirmation to the client | 30 min |
| 3 | If the payment hasn't arrived: send **one** reminder to anyone who said yes (Message 7), and **10 more first messages** | 60 min |
| 4 | If a client has paid: start delivery — create the tracker folder, import their enquiries, and send them a message confirming their delivery dates | 90 min |
| 5 | End-of-week review: fill in the weekly summary in `01-research-and-validation/interview-log.md` | 25 min |

**Output:** either a paid client in delivery, or 40+ cumulative contacts and a clear, written decision about what to change next week.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Payment verified **in my own app** (not a screenshot)
- [ ] Invoice sent
- [ ] Onboarding form sent
- [ ] Delivery dates confirmed to the client in writing
- [ ] Client's tracker folder created
- [ ] Weekly summary completed with real numbers
- [ ] Decision made and written down: continue / change offer / change audience

**If it doesn't work:**
- **No payment yet, but warm conversations:** this is normal. Warm conversations convert over 2–3 weeks. Keep the pipeline warm, keep sending new messages, and set specific follow-up dates. **Do not** drop your price in panic.
- **Nothing at all after 40 messages:** the decision rules (`validation-experiment.md` §7) apply. Most likely it's a channel problem. One week of email + website forms only, with a completely different first line.
- **You feel like quitting:** reread `01-research-and-validation/idea-assessment.md` §3. The most common outcome of week one is a lot of silence. Judge the week by **contacts made (40+)** and **conversations (5+)**, not by payments.

## What "success" looks like at each level

| Level | Outcome | How to feel about it |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum acceptable** | 40+ contacts, 5+ conversations, 3+ explicit offers | You did the work. That is the only thing you control |
| **Good** | Everything above, plus 2+ people who asked how to pay | You have a real pipeline. Expect a payment within 1–2 weeks |
| **Target** | One ₹400 advance received and verified | Validation. Now deliver brilliantly |
| **Exceptional** | 2–3 payments | You have a small business. Raise the price to ₹1,499 for client #4 |

---

## The seven habits that decide whether this week works

1. **Send messages before doing admin.** Every single day.
2. **Personalise every first line.** This is 80% of your reply rate.
3. **One follow-up, then stop.** Nobody owes you an answer.
4. **Never lie, never exaggerate, never invent urgency.** Your reputation in a small community is your only asset.
5. **Ask for the money clearly.** "Shall we do it? Here's the link." Not "let me know if you're interested."
6. **Log everything the same day.** Your memory will rewrite the week.
7. **Judge yourself on inputs, not outcomes.** Contacts sent, conversations had, offers made. Payments are the result of those, not a substitute for them.

## Beyond day 7

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| Week 2 | Deliver client #1 excellently. Ask for a testimonial and one referral. Keep 10 outreach messages going daily |
| Week 3 | Client #2. Time the delivery, cut the hours, update your templates |
| Week 4 | Client #3. Then, and only then, decide whether to raise the price, add the monthly retainer, or build any automation |

**Remember the honest position, always:** you are selling clarity and follow-up discipline. You are not selling bookings, leads, or guarantees. Say it plainly, deliver what you promised, and delete their data on time. That is a business worth having.

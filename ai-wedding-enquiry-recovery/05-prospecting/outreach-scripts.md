# Outreach Scripts

Short, human, ready to send. **Every first message needs one specific observation about their work or process** — otherwise you are just another DM.

**Non-negotiable rules:** identify yourself · one specific detail · one clear ask · say how to make you stop · one follow-up maximum · instant opt-out.

---

## 1. Instagram DM — first message (use this most)

> Hi `[Name]` — I'm `[Your name]`, I work with wedding photographers on the follow-up side of enquiries (not a photographer myself).
>
> I noticed your bio says "DM for bookings". Genuine question: when 30–40 of those chats are open at once, how do you keep track of who still needs a reply? Most photographers I've asked say that's the part that slips.
>
> I built a small 7-day service that sorts existing enquiries into one tracker and writes the follow-ups for you. Happy to send the sample so you can judge — and if this isn't relevant, just say so and I won't message again.

**Why it works:** it asks about *their* reality, offers something to look at, and gives them a painless exit.

## 3. Business email — subject lines that get opened

Pick one:
- `Your "DM for bookings" — quick question`
- `Enquiries after the price list goes out`
- `I organise wedding enquiries — sample attached?`
- `Follow-ups for `[Studio name]` — 7-day pilot (₹799)`

**Avoid:** "Partnership opportunity", "Revolutionary AI solution", "Collaboration proposal", "Grow your business 10x".

>
> What I do instead: every message is written, personalised and ready. You copy it, change anything you want, and send it from your own number. It takes about 30 seconds per couple, and it stays your relationship.

## When to contact, when to follow up, when to stop

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| **Best days** | Tuesday–Thursday |
| **Best hours** | 10 am–1 pm, or 4–6 pm (photographers are usually not shooting then) |
| **Avoid** | Weekends, festival days, after 9 pm, and the week of a major wedding date in their city |
| **First message** | Once, on one channel |
| **Follow-up** | Once, 4 days later, **same channel** |
| **Third message** | Never, unless they responded |
| **They replied anything** | Reply within a few hours during business hours — speed reads as reliability |
| **They said no / stop / blocked** | Never again. Mark `DO NOT CONTACT` |
| **No reply after 2 messages** | Mark `Not now`, set a nurture date 60–90 days out |
| **They went quiet mid-conversation** | One message after 5 days, then stop |
| **New enquiry from a past "not now"** | Treat it as a fresh, warm conversation |

## The three sentences that make cold outreach work far better

Copy these into your own words — but keep the structure:

1. **"I'm not a photographer."** *(Honesty builds instant credibility because every other DM pretends to be an expert.)*
2. **"Most photographers I've asked say the follow-ups are the part that slips."** *(Normative, non-accusatory, invites them to agree or correct you.)*
3. **"If this isn't relevant, tell me and I won't message again."** *(Removes the fear of an endless drip, which is why people block instead of replying.)*

**Never** write: "You're losing customers", "I noticed you're not following up", "You're missing out on ₹X", "Guaranteed bookings", or anything that implies you know their business better than they do.
